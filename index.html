About this Website

I decided it would be useful to bring together in one place a selection of the various works, documents, and other material I have generated over the years.

The original purpose of this website was fairly narrow: to compile the most relevant documents relating to my litigation with Westsea, so that the record could be readily accessed in one place. From there, it seemed sensible to add a section linking to my published academic papers.

At that point, I decided I might as well go the whole nine yards and include other material I have produced over the years—research, writing, poetry, music, photographs, and whatever else seems worth preserving or sharing.

This is not intended to be an exhaustive archive. The threshold is simply that the material should, in my own judgment, be of reasonable quality or continuing interest. That criterion will, fortunately, filter out quite a lot.
